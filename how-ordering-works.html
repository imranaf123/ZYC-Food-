How Ordering Works

ZYC Food orders are placed and confirmed on WhatsApp, so there's no account to create and no order history stored on this site. Here's the whole flow.

STEP 01

Build your order

Pick your items, choose sizes, set the spice level and add any extras. The price updates as you go.

STEP 02

Delivery or pickup

Choose pickup for free collection, or set your distance and see the delivery fee update instantly.

STEP 03

Send on WhatsApp

Your full order — items, options, address and total — is written into a WhatsApp message for you.

STEP 04

We confirm & cook

Our team replies on WhatsApp with a confirmation and an ETA, then the kitchen gets to work.

No order history here. Because this site has no customer accounts, past orders live in your WhatsApp chat with us — that's your receipt and your tracking thread.

Need an update on a live order?

Message the same WhatsApp thread you ordered on, or give the kitchen a call.
